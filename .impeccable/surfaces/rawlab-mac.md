# RawLab Mac Editor

Mode: Operate.

Photographers browse RAW and raster photos, adjust a selected photo and export while preserving the source. The native workspace puts folder navigation and thumbnails on the left, the photo in the center and a histogram and scrolling adjustment inspector on the right. In a narrow window the browser opens over the canvas. The toolbar switches between editing, contact-sheet selection and a full-width preview.

Success means a photographer can keep a photo open while browsing and collapsing a large directory, send a selection to the existing batch workflow, and make precise changes in the inspector. Look Strength is a shared control above the Looks tools. Highlights and Shadows center on zero. Color Grading exposes three tonal zones with scoped resets. Geometry and lens edits have explicit draft actions.

[DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) records dimensions and the native interaction rules. SwiftUI and AppKit provide the production interface.
