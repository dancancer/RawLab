RawLab Android uses the repository's shared Sony2Fuji C++ core.

LibRaw 0.22.2: https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/tree/0.22.2
Source archive: https://www.libraw.org/data/LibRaw-0.22.2.tar.gz
SHA-256: de86b035655accff8d4010f1a221fdf50d353cb7b1422ba26f14a0db92612cfa
Built from patched source as libraw.so; Android CMake build configuration
is supplied in app/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt. LGPL/CDDL license choices and
copyright notices are included in this directory. Optional LCMS, JPEG/JasPer,
RawSpeed and Adobe DNG SDK integrations are not compiled into LibRaw here.

Local crop, X-Trans concurrency and Nikon recognition changes are applied by
lutools/cmake/patch-libraw.cmake. Four exact-model camera matrices are adapted
from RawSpeed contributors' cameras.xml at:
https://github.com/darktable-org/rawspeed/blob/c835b05aecfacb7343f7c424abd620aa12116c3f/data/cameras.xml
The adapted data remains under CC BY-SA 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
Source: lutools/third_party/rawspeed-camera-calibration.inc in the RawLab repository.
Changes: extraction into LibRaw table syntax; black/white levels remain metadata-driven.
No RawSpeed decoder code is included.
R6 III layout/matrix evidence is from ErikCJohansson's contribution at
https://github.com/LibRaw/LibRaw/issues/821; the parser reuses LibRaw v12 offsets.
This local correction does not imply upstream R6 III support.

The core's temperature/xy calculation includes Adobe DNG SDK-derived code;
its notice is provided as Adobe-DNG-SDK-LICENSE.txt. Embedded JPEG reading and
JPEG output use bundled stb headers; their notices are included separately.
zlib is provided by the Android system, not bundled as a separate library.
libc++_shared.so is supplied by Android NDK 27.2.12479018; its license is
included as NDK-libcxx-NOTICE in the generated APK assets.

Film LUTs and generated film-label artwork are reused unchanged from this
repository. The artwork is not an official product image or brand endorsement.
See RawLabMac/Resources/FilmIcons/generated-set-a.md and generated-set-b.md
for artwork provenance. Film output follows lutools/docs/color-contract.md.
